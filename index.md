# The Societal Analytics Lab

Hey there! Welcome! 

The Societal Analytics Lab aims to facilitate the use of computational resources 
by developing infrastructure and knowledge relevant to social science research.

Our projects cover tools to facilitate data validation [AmCAT](https://amcat4.labs.vu.nl/), 
data annotation [AnnoTinder](https://annocate.com/), and more!

Do you have a software need? Or do you have a software idea? Then come to us! We
would be happy to either develop the software for you, or to help you get started!

As an example, you can check [Scripttorium](https://github.com/Rptkiddle/scriptorium), 
which started as an idea by [Rupert Kiddle](https://research.vu.nl/en/persons/rupert-kiddle/) and 
now it is also part of our projects.
