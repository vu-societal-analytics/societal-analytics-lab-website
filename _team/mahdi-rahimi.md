Mahdi Rahimi – Lab Research Engineer at the Societal Analytics Lab.

One of his main tasks is working on OPTIMA, the Observatory for Political Texts, Images, and Multimedia, an open science infrastructure where researchers can securely store, analyze, annotate, and share political communication data. To do this, Mahdi together with us will be extending and integrating two of the lab’s open-source platforms, AmCAT and AnnoTinder.

Mahdi holds a PhD in GIS from K.N. Toosi University of Technology. Before joining VU, he led quantitative analysis and data pipeline development for the Horizon Europe project AccessCity4All at the University of Groningen. His expertise includes research software engineering, data modelling, knowledge graphs, network science, location-based social networks, spatial analysis, and large-scale data pipelines in R, Python, and SQL.
