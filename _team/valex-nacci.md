# About my project

*Internship supervisor:* [Dr. Sofia Gil-Clavel](https://www.sofiagilclavel.com/)

*Project:* Climate obstruction in Meta Ads: A closer look at Mexico’s regional 
climate communication

*Abstract:* The use of climate obstruction strategies has been studied primarily 
in Europe and the United States.  In this project the focus is moved to Mexico, 
where climate discourse has been analyzed to reveal the regional differences 
between Quintana Roo, a tourism-dependent state, and Oaxaca, a region with strong 
community-based conservation traditions. The analysis was conducted on Meta-ads 
published in Mexico during 2025.

The project aims to show how climate discourse can be strongly influenced by 
local power structures and dependencies.
