# About my project

*Project:* Mapping the Environmental Impact of Generative AI

*Abstract:* While generative AI technologies are becoming increasingly pervasive 
in daily life, users often lack awareness regarding their true environmental 
footprints. As a result, we focus on mapping the global environmental cost/impact 
of AI development using automated text analysis, in hope to turn it into an interactive 
product to help individuals clearly see the sustainability challenge behind AI 
development and usage.
