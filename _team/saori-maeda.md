Saori Maeda – Research Intern.

# About me

Saori Maeda is a Research Master’s student in Social Sciences for a Digital 
Society at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and a research intern at the Societal 
Analytics Lab. Her current work focuses on computational methods for analysing 
digital behaviour, including longitudinal media diary data, LLM-assisted text 
classification, and the integration of self-reported data with donated digital
trace data.

Her broader research interests concern how the use of LLMs in decision-making 
may generate or amplify group-level disparities, particularly in public-sector 
and policy contexts.

She holds a Master of Economics from the University of Tokyo and previously 
worked as a visiting researcher at the Policy Research Institute, Ministry of 
Finance, Japan.

# About my project

*Internship supervisor:* [Dr. Kasper Welbers](https://research.vu.nl/en/persons/kasper-welbers/)

*Project:* Validating LLM-Assisted Coding of Longitudinal Media Diary Data

*Abstract:* This project investigates the use of large language models (LLMs) 
for coding longitudinal open-ended media diary data. It compares LLM-assisted 
classification with human coding and examines whether coding performance varies 
across response characteristics such as language, response length, survey wave, 
and political topic. The project further explores whether differences between 
human and LLM coding affect substantive conclusions drawn from longitudinal 
analyses. Where feasible, coded diary measures will also be linked to donated 
digital trace data to study the relationship between self-reported media 
experiences and observed digital behaviour. By evaluating both classification 
performance and its implications for downstream analysis, the project contributes 
to methodological discussions on the valid and responsible use of LLMs in 
computational social science.
