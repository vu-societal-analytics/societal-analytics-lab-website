## Description

A common problem when teaching R is that lecturers have to deal with students' different operating systems.
This creates a lot of friction between the course intended outcomes and the amount of time
lecturers have to spend solving students' R installations.

If you recognized yourself, then this course is for you!

Besides that problem, we will also cover:

* How to standardize students' homework. So that, it is easier to discover fraud and to grade!
* How to make the best use of VU resources to teach R.
* How to publish your course materials with a DOI, while performin version control, e.g. [https://zenodo.org/records/20828330](https://zenodo.org/records/20828330).
